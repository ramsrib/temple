## What's new

- **A redesigned sidebar.** Each project is a quiet uppercase label with a hairline rule and a chevron that turns as it folds. Open sessions read at full strength; the closed ones behind them step back a tone. Hover and selection are soft pills, and folding a project animates within its group.
- **Search, add project and the sidebar toggle live in the title bar**, together by the traffic lights, and stay put when the sidebar hides. The magnifier unfolds the search field, which folds away again when empty. The footer is one line: meters and the gear.
- **Color marks show in ⌘K, the ⌃⇥ tab switcher and ⌘Y**, as a wash on the row, so a marked tab is easy to spot where you look for it.
- **The dot that pulses is the one waiting on you.** A session that needs your attention breathes; a running one holds still.
