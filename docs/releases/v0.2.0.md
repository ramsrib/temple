## What's new

- **Archive sessions and projects.** Archive a session from its row's menu, or a whole project from its header. It leaves the sidebar, ⌘K, ⌘Y, the home page and the ⌘N picker; nothing on disk is deleted. Archiving a session unpins it, and ⌘Z (Edit ▸ Undo Archive) undoes both. Archiving is refused while a tab is open, and opening an archived session brings it back.
- **An archive browser on ⌘⇧Y.** Laid out like the sidebar: archived projects with every session they hold, then sessions archived on their own under their project's name. Search by folder or session, Restore a project or a session (also undoable), or open a session to restore it on the way. The home page has an Archived items row, and View has Archived Items.
- **Reorder projects by dragging their headers.** An insertion line shows where the project lands, and the order is kept.

## Upgrading

- The first launch migrates the database (one table added for project order and archived projects). Nothing is deleted.
