## What's new

- **⌘F finds text in the terminal.** A compact find bar opens over the terminal's top-right corner and searches the scrollback. Return or ⌘G goes to the next match, ⇧Return or ⌘⇧G to the previous, and Esc closes it. The search stays with its tab when you switch away. The Edit menu has Find in Terminal, Find Next and Find Previous.
- **The sidebar comes back the way you left it.** Hidden or shown, it stays that way across a relaunch.
- Sidebar search no longer has a shortcut; click the field to search.

## Fixes

- ⌘B could do nothing on the first press when the sidebar was already open.

## Upgrading

- The first launch migrates the database (one table added for window state). Nothing is deleted.
- ⌘F now searches the terminal instead of focusing the sidebar search.
