## What's new

- **Session history on ⌘Y.** Every session, newest first, grouped under Today, Yesterday and earlier dates. Type to search. ⌘K, ⌘Y and ⌘/ replace each other rather than stacking.
- **⌘⇧T reopens the last closed tab.**
- **⌘N starts a session somewhere you name.** A picker over every project, most recent first, with Choose folder… at the end. ⌘⇧N does the same with your other agent. The home page moves to ⌘⇧H.
- **Color marks and in-place rename for tabs.** Right-click a chip to give it one of seven colors, which tint the chip and mark its sidebar row, and survive closing and relaunching. Double-click a chip to rename it.
- **A subscription usage meter** in the sidebar footer: your current Claude and Codex usage as one percentage each, with the full breakdown on hover. When there is no login or the data isn't there, it shows nothing.
- **A native menu bar.** File holds the session commands, View the sidebar, palette, history and home, a new Project menu mirrors the switcher, and Settings… sits in the app menu. macOS window tabbing is off, so its second tab bar can't appear.
- **Dragging tabs feels like a browser.** The chip follows the cursor, neighbors slide aside, and the strip scrolls at its edges for long moves. Tabs activate on the first click.
- **Closing a tab returns you to the tab you were on before**, not the first one in the row.
- **⌘K finds sessions by the name you see.** Search matches the displayed title (a rename or the agent's own title) as well as the original prompt. With nothing typed, ⌘K lists open sessions, most recent first.
- **One right-click menu.** Chip and sidebar menus offer the same actions in the same order, including Pin, Reveal in Finder and the colors. The sidebar footer gear opens a small menu: Settings…, Keyboard Shortcuts, About Temple.
