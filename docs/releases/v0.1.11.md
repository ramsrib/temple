## Fixes

- **The usage meter no longer freezes on old numbers.** One failed Keychain read could stop the Claude reading for the life of the app while the meter kept showing the last figures. Clicking refresh in the usage card now tries again, and the card says "Couldn't refresh" once Claude has missed three updates.
- Opening the usage card no longer asks for Keychain access again after you denied it; only the refresh button does.
- Tab chips fill the title bar's full height instead of floating in it.
