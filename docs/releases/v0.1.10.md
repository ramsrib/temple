## What's new

- **⌃⇥ walks your tabs most recently used first.** Hold ⌃ and tap ⇥ to move through a switcher of open tabs, across projects; release ⌃ to land. One tap bounces to the tab you were just on. ⌃⇧⇥ and the arrows walk back, Return lands, Esc cancels. ⌘1 to ⌘9 still pick by position.
- **Move to Front and Move to End** in a chip's context menu, for crowded tab rows.

## Fixes

- A tab drag interrupted by Mission Control or the app losing focus no longer leaves the chip stuck mid-drag.
- Switching tabs or projects with a held modifier no longer leaves the terminal thinking a key is still pressed.
