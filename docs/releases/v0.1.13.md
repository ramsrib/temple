## What's new

- **Relaunch reopens the tab you left.** Temple used to restore your tabs and then land on the home page. Now it activates the tab that was active, and resumes only that one agent. If you quit from the home page, you come back to it.
- **Closing the window quits Temple.** The red button used to leave a windowless app with agents running out of sight, which were then killed without a clean shutdown when a new window opened. Closing now does what ⌘Q does: agents are shut down cleanly, your tabs are saved, and they come back next launch.
- **Quitting asks while an agent is working.** Idle sessions never prompt; they resume from disk with nothing lost. ⌘Q is now listed in the ⌘/ card.

## Upgrading

- The first launch migrates the database (one column added to remember the active tab). Nothing is deleted.

## Known issue

- Choosing Cancel on the prompt after clicking the red close button still quits, ending the working agent. ⌘Q's prompt is not affected. Fixed in v0.1.14.
