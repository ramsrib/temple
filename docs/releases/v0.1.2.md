## What's new

- **Real title bar tabs.** The tab strip now spans the title bar from the sidebar to the window edge, with browser-style chips, the project switcher on the left, + on the right, and ‹ › arrows when tabs overflow. Drag to reorder works in the title bar.
- **⌘P switches projects like ⌘⇥ switches apps.** Hold ⌘ and tap P to walk the projects you have open, most recent first; release ⌘ to land. One tap bounces to the project you were just in.
- **Open a folder Temple has never seen** from the sidebar's Projects header or the project switcher, without going back to the home page.
- **Codex exec sessions get real titles.** Sessions started with `codex exec` used to read "(no prompt)"; they are now titled from the session index or the rollout's first prompt.
- **Panels look and behave as one.** ⌘K, ⌘P and the ⌘/ shortcuts card share one opaque panel style, and the shortcuts card shows keycaps beside plain actions.
- **The sidebar drops its scrollbar**, which "Show scroll bars: Always" turned into a permanent bar beside every row.

## Fixes

- Panels could be visible but not clickable, with clicks landing on the page beneath. They now take their own clicks, and a click outside dismisses them.
- Typing right after launch no longer lands in the sidebar search.
- About shows the right version. v0.1.1 reported itself as 0.1.0.
